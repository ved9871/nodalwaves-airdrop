FAQ

Straight answers.

Why only $10?

Because the point is a meaningful first step, not a large one. A $10 qualifying purchase keeps the entry accessible while still being a real, committed position inside the ecosystem.

Do I receive $20?

No. The promotional benefit is an additional NODAL token-unit allocation based on the qualifying purchased token quantity. It is not a guaranteed additional $10 of future value. NODAL's market value can change in either direction.

What is promotional NODAL?

An additional allocation of NODAL equal to the qualifying token quantity you purchased at activation. It is held in a separate allocation and released under the program's release policy. It is a token quantity, not a fixed dollar amount.

Is future NODAL value guaranteed?

No. Nobody can promise what a token will be worth. Crypto assets can rise or fall in value, so only participate with an amount you are comfortable committing for the full 24 months.

Is NODAL company equity?

No. NODAL is the participation token of the NodalWaves ecosystem. It is not company shares, equity, or a claim on NodalWaves revenue or profits.

What does the 24-month commitment mean?

Your qualifying NODAL stays committed (locked) for 24 months from activation. The program is designed around longer-term participation, so plan on the full period. The detailed conditions are in the program terms.

Do I have to invite other people?

No. Your $10 participation and promotional allocation stand on their own. The Community Partner program is optional and only for people who choose to opt in.

What is Nodal Passport?

Your digital identity inside the NodalWaves ecosystem. It is where your participation, progress, badges and achievements come together in one place.

What is Nodal Quest?

The learning and progression layer of the ecosystem. Complete quests to learn how NodalWaves works, earn XP and badges, and build your progress over time.

What could I explore inside the wider NodalWaves ecosystem?

Staking and participation, Nodes, marketplace and utility features, and future gaming and digital experiences as they launch. Layers roll out in phases and availability may change, so read the ecosystem map as a direction, not a promise.
