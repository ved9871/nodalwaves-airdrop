The match

Token for token.
Not dollar for dollar.

Whatever quantity of $NODAL your $10 buys at the live quote, the program allocates the same quantity again as promotional NODAL. Same token. Same decimals. A separate vault, released under the 24-month policy.

The quantity is fixed at activation. The price is not. That's the whole honest version, and it's the only version you'll see on this page.

Qualified means activated

A sign-up isn't a direct. Someone counts only when their $10 purchase settles, their NODAL commits and their promo allocates.

No spillover, no compression

Nobody is auto-placed under you and no one's unearned level jumps to someone else. What you build is yours. What you haven't built goes to reserve.

A rule, not a forecast

The figures above show the rate applied to one example activation. They aren't a projection of what you'll receive. Community allocations are separate from any staking policy and are claimable under the program's claim rules.

Straight talk

What this is. What it isn't.

You'll read the fine print anyway, so here it is in big type.

This is

  • A $10 purchase of $NODAL that commits for 24 months.
  • A token-for-token promo allocation, the same quantity you bought, in a separate locked vault.
  • A five-level community allocation, 5 / 5 / 4 / 3 / 3, capped at 20%, only if you opt in as a Community Partner.
  • One promo per verified device. Extra purchases are welcome; they don't mint extra promos.
  • A receipt for everything. Source, generation, rate, quantity, status.

This isn't

  • A promise of $20. The match is a token quantity. Its market value moves.
  • Guaranteed income, a fixed rate, or a price call. None of that exists here, and nobody official will tell you otherwise.
  • A forced matrix. No spillover, no compression, no auto-placement.
  • A cash balance. Promo NODAL sits in its vault until the release policy says otherwise.
  • Staking. Any staking benefit follows its own policy and never mixes with the promo.

FAQ

Ask the awkward ones.

Why 24 months?

Because the program is built for people who want to be in the ecosystem, not through it. Your purchased NODAL commits for 24 months from activation. The promo allocation follows its own release policy tied to the same window. Both dates are on your dashboard from day one.

Do I have to bring five people?

No. The $10 activation and the 1:1 promo match stand on their own. Build your 5 is an opt-in Community Partner role for people who want to grow a network. Skip it and nothing about your activation changes.

Can I have more than five directs?

Yes. Five is the qualification target, not a ceiling. Every qualified direct beyond five still activates in your generation 1 and still counts toward your ranks.

What if my payment fails?

Then nothing happens, on purpose. No commitment, no promo, no direct qualification, no upline allocation. The activation is one atomic sequence, and a settlement that reverts leaves no half-finished state behind.

Why only one promo per device?

Because the promo is for real people, not account farms. Eligibility is tracked per verified device with wallet and behaviour signals on top. Suspicious cases go to a human review queue with a reference number you can quote.

What is $NODAL, anyway?

The Nodal Token: 10 billion fixed supply on Polygon, 0% founder or team allocation. It's the participation token of the NodalWaves ecosystem: staking commitment, Lite and Founder Nodes, Node-Linked Vaults, NodalQuest and the products on the roadmap after them.

Read the litepaper at nodalwaves.com before you activate. Do your own research. Never spend what you can't afford to lock away.
