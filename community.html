Straight talk

What this is. What it isn't.

This is

  • An optional role you choose from inside your own participation
  • Allocations in token units, per qualifying activation, in the generations you have unlocked
  • Capped at 20% per qualifying activation across all five generations
  • A separate ledger from your purchased and promotional NODAL, with a receipt for every line

This isn't

  • Guaranteed income, a salary, or a fixed rate of return
  • A requirement for your own $10 participation or promotional NODAL
  • A promise of a future token price
  • A reason to pressure anyone. Share what NodalWaves is building, honestly, and let people decide

Community allocations require Community Partner opt-in and are subject to eligibility, caps and claim rules. Crypto assets can rise or fall in value. Read the program terms before taking part.

FAQ

Partner questions.

Do I have to bring five people?

No. Your own $10 participation and promotional NODAL stand on their own. Community Partner is an opt-in role for people who want to build a community. Skip it and nothing about your participation changes.

What counts as a qualified direct?

Someone who completed the full activation sequence: their qualifying purchase settled, their NODAL committed for the participation period and their promotional allocation completed. A registration on its own qualifies nothing.

Are community allocations guaranteed?

No. They depend on qualifying activations actually happening in the generations you have unlocked. The rates describe how the program allocates when they do. They are never a projection of what you will receive, and their market value can change.

When can I use community allocations?

Community allocations are recorded in their own ledger and are claimable under the program's claim rules, which are published with the program terms.

How should I talk about NodalWaves?

Plainly. Describe the ecosystem, the $10 entry, the 24-month commitment and the promotional allocation as they are. Never promise income, a token price or a return. If you would not say it to a friend, don't say it at all.
