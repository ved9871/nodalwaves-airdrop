The one message.

Start small.
Enter the ecosystem.

Everything in the launch points at a single idea: Nodal Gateway is the entry into the NodalWaves ecosystem, and it can start with $10 in NODAL. We sell the ecosystem and the first step. We do not sell the $10, the promotion, or any outcome.

If a post cannot be traced back to that sentence, it does not go out.

03

Language rules.

Non-negotiable

Say

  • Nodal Gateway, the NodalWaves ecosystem, NODAL
  • "a qualifying $10 purchase of NODAL"
  • "committed for the 24-month participation period"
  • "promotional NODAL" or "an additional allocation equal to the quantity you purchased"
  • "layers roll out in phases; features, availability and timing may change"
  • "Crypto assets involve risk. No guaranteed value or return."

Never say

  • match, 1:1, matched, double, "$10 becomes $20", free money
  • airdrop, giveaway (the allocation is purchase-linked, see 11)
  • any future price, target, multiple or return
  • income, earnings, passive income, financial freedom, get rich
  • guaranteed, risk-free, safe investment
  • rank names, percentages or level counts on launch channels

Tone

Short sentences.One idea each. Read it out loud before it goes.
No exclamation marksin brand posts. The calm is the point.
No emojisexcept a single arrow or dot where a platform needs a visual cue.
No countdown pressure.No "only 24 hours", no "last chance".

Rewrite examples

Came in like this
Goes out like this
Get matched 1:1 and double your NODAL
Receive promotional NODAL equal to the quantity you purchased
$10 today could be worth so much more
$10 can be your first committed NODAL position
Don't miss the airdrop, last chance!
The entry is open. Start small. Enter the ecosystem.
Earn passive income with your network
Your own participation never depends on inviting anyone
Every caption ends with the risk line. One call to action per post, and it is always the site.

Partners who change the words keep the risk line. If you would not say it to a friend sitting across from you, do not post it.

Launch-day checklist.

  • gateway.nodalwaves.com loads on mobile and desktop; the calculator shows live numbers; Start opens the entry page
  • Tracked links tested; partner referral links tested
  • All three videos play with the sound off and still make sense; subtitles attached where supported
  • Captions pasted and reviewed against the language rules
  • Telegram pin written, stay-safe line included
  • Support replies loaded into the help desk

  • Someone is watching comments and DMs for the first six hours
  • Impersonation watch: search for fake accounts and lookalike links, report immediately

What we measure.

Reach is not the goal. Understanding is.
  • Site visits per channel (UTMs) and time on page
  • Scroll depth to the "No hype" section
  • Starts of the entry flow, from the gateway app's dashboard
  • Questions asked in comments and DMs, and how many are answered within an hour
  • Saves and shares of poster 05. When the honest post travels, the launch is working
Weekly review: what question came up most, and does the page or the FAQ answer it? If not, fix the page before posting more.

11

Why we never say airdrop.

Promotional NODAL
is purchase-linked.

An airdrop gives tokens away for nothing. Nodal Gateway's promotional NODAL is tied to a qualifying purchase and a 24-month commitment. Calling it an airdrop would attract people expecting free tokens and set up a complaint later.

We call it promotional NODAL, we describe it as an additional allocation equal to the quantity purchased, and we leave it there. The copy pack has a ready reply for anyone who asks.

If something goes wrong.

Site outagePause scheduled posts, post a one-line status in Telegram, resume when green
Press asks about returns or price targetsDecline the question, offer the factsheet language, no speculation
