# Nodal Gateway · social launch playbook

How to run the launch of gateway.nodalwaves.com across social, community and press, with the assets in this kit. Written for the person posting, the person approving, and the partners who share.

---

## 1. The one message

**Start small. Enter the ecosystem.**

Everything in the launch points at a single idea: Nodal Gateway is the entry into the NodalWaves ecosystem, and it can start with $10 in NODAL. We sell the ecosystem and the first step. We do not sell the $10, the promotion, or any outcome.

If a post cannot be traced back to that sentence, it does not go out.

## 2. Who we are talking to

Digitally native people, roughly 18 to 30, on their phones, comfortable with apps and games, maybe curious about crypto but tired of being shouted at by it. They have small budgets and good instincts for hype. Every asset is built to pass their first-glance test: calm, clear, specific, honest about risk.

## 3. Language rules (non-negotiable)

Say
- Nodal Gateway, the NodalWaves ecosystem, NODAL
- "a qualifying $10 purchase of NODAL"
- "committed for the 24-month participation period"
- "promotional NODAL" or "an additional allocation equal to the quantity you purchased"
- "the ecosystem rolls out in phases; features, availability and timing may change"
- "Crypto assets involve risk. No guaranteed value or return."

Never say
- match, 1:1, matched, double, "$10 becomes $20", free money
- airdrop, giveaway (the allocation is purchase-linked; see section 11)
- any future price, target, multiple or return
- income, earnings, passive income, financial freedom, get rich
- guaranteed, risk-free, safe investment
- rank names, percentages or level counts on the main launch channels (Community Partner details stay on the community page)

Tone
- Short sentences. One idea each.
- No exclamation marks in brand posts.
- No emojis in captions except a single arrow or dot where a platform needs a visual cue.
- No countdown pressure ("only 24 hours", "last chance").

| Role | Owns |

| Launch lead | Calendar, go/no-go, final approval on every post |
| Community lead | Telegram, WhatsApp, DMs, support replies, partner note |
| Content lead | Scheduling, captions, SRT upload, asset QA |
| Compliance reviewer | Language rules check on every caption before it is scheduled |

Approval flow: content lead drafts → compliance reviewer checks against section 3 → launch lead approves → scheduled. Nothing is posted live from a phone without going through this.

## 9. Launch-day checklist

- [ ] gateway.nodalwaves.com loads on mobile and desktop; calculator shows live numbers; Start goes to the entry page
- [ ] Tracked links tested; `?ref=` partner links tested
- [ ] All three videos play with sound off and make sense; SRT attached where supported
- [ ] Captions pasted and reviewed against the language rules
- [ ] Telegram pin written; stay-safe line included
- [ ] Support replies (messages.md) loaded into the help desk
- [ ] Press release and factsheet sent to the media list, embargo lifted at launch time
- [ ] Someone is watching comments and DMs for the first six hours
- [ ] Impersonation watch: search for fake accounts and lookalike links; report immediately

## 10. What we measure

Reach is not the goal. Understanding is. Watch, in this order:
1. Site visits per channel (UTMs) and time on page
2. Scroll depth to the "No hype" section
3. Starts of the entry flow (from the gateway app's dashboard)
4. Questions asked in comments and DMs, and how many are answered within an hour
5. Saves and shares of poster 05 (a healthy sign that the honest post travels)

Weekly review: what question came up most, and does the page or the FAQ answer it? If not, fix the page before posting more.

## 11. Why we never say "airdrop"

An airdrop gives tokens away for nothing. Nodal Gateway's promotional NODAL is tied to a qualifying purchase and a 24-month commitment. Calling it an airdrop would attract people expecting free tokens and set up a complaint later. We call it promotional NODAL, we describe it as an additional allocation equal to the quantity purchased, and we leave it there.

## 12. If something goes wrong

| Situation | Do |
|---|---|
| A post slips through with banned language | Delete, repost corrected, note it in the weekly review |
| Impersonator or fake link | Report on platform, post the stay-safe line in Telegram, pin it |
| Price question in comments | Reply with the support line on price; do not engage further |
| Site outage | Pause scheduled posts; post a one-line status in Telegram; resume when green |
| Press asks about returns or price targets | Decline the question, offer the factsheet language, no speculation |
