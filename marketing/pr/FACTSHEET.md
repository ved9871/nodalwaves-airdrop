## How it works

1. **Get NODAL.** Make a qualifying $10 purchase of NODAL at the applicable price. The quantity is confirmed at execution.
2. **Commit.** Keep the qualifying NODAL committed for the 24-month participation period.
3. **Receive promotional NODAL.** An additional allocation equal to the qualifying purchased token quantity, held as a separate allocation and committed for the same period.

## What it is / what it is not

**It is**
- A low-entry participation pathway into the NodalWaves ecosystem
- A 24-month commitment

**It is not**
- Guaranteed income
- Guaranteed token appreciation
- Company shares or equity
- A promise of a future token price
- An airdrop or free distribution (the allocation is tied to a qualifying purchase)

## Media FAQ

**Why $10?**
Because the point is a meaningful first step, not a large one. $10 keeps the entry accessible while still being a real, committed position.

**Why 24 months?**
The program is designed around longer-term participation rather than short-term speculation. The commitment period filters for people who want to be part of the ecosystem as it is built.

**Does a participant receive $20?**
No. The promotional benefit is an additional NODAL token-unit allocation based on the quantity purchased. It is not a guaranteed extra $10 of future value. NODAL's market value can change in either direction.

**Is this an airdrop?**
No. An airdrop distributes tokens for free. Promotional NODAL is linked to a qualifying purchase and a 24-month commitment.

**Does a participant have to recruit others?**
No. Individual participation and the promotional allocation stand alone. The Community Partner program is optional and documented on its own page.

**Is NODAL equity?**
No. NODAL is the participation token of the ecosystem. It is not shares, equity, or a claim on revenue or profits.

**What will NODAL be worth?**
NodalWaves does not comment on price or returns. Crypto assets can rise or fall in value.

**How is the estimated quantity on the site calculated?**
$10 divided by the current reference price, shown as an estimate. The final quantity depends on the execution price at purchase.
