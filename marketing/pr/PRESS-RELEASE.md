## NodalWaves opens Nodal Gateway, a $10 entry point into its Web3 ecosystem

*Start small. Enter the ecosystem. The new gateway pairs a low entry with plain-language terms and a 24-month commitment.*

**[City], [Date]** · NodalWaves today opened Nodal Gateway (gateway.nodalwaves.com), the entry point into the NodalWaves ecosystem. The program lets a person begin with a qualifying $10 purchase of NODAL, the ecosystem's participation token on Polygon, and is built around a simple idea: meaningful participation in Web3 should not require a large budget or a technical vocabulary to begin.

Nodal Gateway works in three steps. A participant makes a qualifying $10 purchase of NODAL, keeps it committed for the 24-month participation period, and receives a promotional NODAL allocation equal to the quantity purchased. From that first step, the participant can build a Nodal Passport, the ecosystem's digital identity, and start Nodal Quest, its learning and progression layer, with staking and participation, Nodes, marketplace and utility features, and future gaming and digital experiences rolling out in phases.

"Most of the industry talks to newcomers in either jargon or hype. We wanted a front door that does neither," said [Name, Title, NodalWaves]. "Nodal Gateway says exactly what it is, exactly what it is not, and asks for a first step small enough that a student can take it and serious enough that it means something. The ecosystem is the point. The $10 just opens the door."

The gateway's landing page carries a section titled "No hype. Know what you're joining." It states that the program is a low-entry participation pathway with a 24-month commitment and a purchase-linked promotional allocation, and that it is not guaranteed income, not guaranteed token appreciation, not company equity, and not a promise of a future token price. A live calculator shows the estimated NODAL quantity for $10 at the current reference price and labels the figure as an estimate.

Participation does not require inviting anyone. A separate, optional Community Partner program exists for people who want to help others understand the ecosystem, and its details are kept on its own page.

Nodal Gateway is live now at **gateway.nodalwaves.com**.
