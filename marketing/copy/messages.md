## Support · canned replies

**Price / value questions**
We do not comment on where the NODAL price might go. Crypto assets can rise or fall in value. The calculator on the page shows an estimate at the current reference price; the final quantity depends on the execution price at the time you purchase.

**"Do I get $20?"**
No. The promotional benefit is an additional NODAL token allocation equal to the quantity you purchased at activation. It is not a guaranteed extra $10 of value.

**"What does the 24-month commitment mean?"**
Your qualifying NODAL stays committed (locked) for 24 months from activation. Promotional NODAL is a separate allocation committed for the same period. Plan on the full period before you start.

**"Do I have to invite people?"**
No. Your own participation and promotional allocation stand on their own. The Community Partner program is optional: https://gateway.nodalwaves.com/community.html

**"Someone messaged me from NodalWaves asking for my seed phrase / a payment"**
That is not us. We never message first, never ask for a seed phrase, and never send a payment address in a DM. Please block and report the account, and only use gateway.nodalwaves.com.

**"Is NODAL equity in the company?"**
No. NODAL is the participation token of the NodalWaves ecosystem. It is not shares, equity, or a claim on revenue or profits.
