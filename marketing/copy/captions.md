# Nodal Gateway launch · captions

Ready-to-paste captions for every asset in the kit. Each one is written to be read by a person scrolling fast, so the first line does the work and the risk line always closes.

Destination for every post: **gateway.nodalwaves.com**
Tracking links are in the playbook (UTM table). Use the tracked link in bios and link stickers; the clean URL can stay in the caption text.

House rules that apply to all captions
- Say "promotional NODAL" or "an additional allocation equal to the quantity you purchased". Never "match", "1:1", "double", "$20" or "free money".
- Never state or hint at a future price, a return, or an income.
- Every caption ends with the risk line: *Crypto assets involve risk. No guaranteed value or return.*
- One call to action per post, and it is always the site.
